## 4.1 Why Get AWS Certified?

AWS certifications are globally recognized credentials that validate your cloud expertise.

- **Career growth** — AWS-certified professionals earn significantly more than non-certified peers
- **Market demand** — AWS holds 32%+ of the global cloud market, making AWS skills among the most sought-after in tech
- **Credibility** — certifications demonstrate real, validated knowledge to employers and clients
- **Learning structure** — the certification path gives you a clear, organized way to build cloud knowledge progressively

---

## 4.2 AWS Certification Levels

AWS organizes its certifications across four levels:

```
AWS Certification Levels
│
├── FOUNDATIONAL (No experience required)
│   ├── AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)  ← Start here
│   └── AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01)     ← AIML students: take this next
│
├── ASSOCIATE (6–12 months recommended experience)
│   ├── Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03)
│   ├── Developer – Associate (DVA-C02)
│   ├── SysOps Administrator – Associate (SOA-C02)
│   └── ML Engineer – Associate (MLA-C01)
│
├── PROFESSIONAL (2+ years recommended experience)
│   ├── Solutions Architect – Professional (SAP-C02)
│   ├── DevOps Engineer – Professional (DOP-C02)
│   └── Generative AI Developer – Professional (AIP-C01, 2026)
│
└── SPECIALTY (Deep domain expertise)
    ├── Advanced Networking
    ├── Security
    ├── Database
    └── Data Engineer – Associate
```

## 4.7 Exam Preparation Tips

### Study Timeline

| Experience Level | Recommended Time |
|---|---|
| Complete beginner | 3–4 months |
| Some cloud exposure | 6–8 weeks |
| Daily AWS user | 2–4 weeks |

### How to Prepare

**Phase 1 — Learn the concepts (40%)**
- Watch the free Skill Builder course for your exam
- Read the AWS documentation for each service in the exam guide

**Phase 2 — Hands-on practice (40%)**
- Use your AWS Free Tier account to build what you're studying
- Follow guided labs at [workshops.aws](https://workshops.aws)

**Phase 3 — Practice exams (20%)**
- Take full-length practice exams under timed conditions
- Review every wrong answer — understand *why* it's wrong
- Aim for **80%+ consistently** before booking the real exam

### Exam Day Tips
- Read carefully — AWS tests the *most appropriate* answer, not just a correct one
- Eliminate clearly wrong answers first — usually 2 of 4 can be ruled out
- Watch for keywords: "most cost-effective", "least operational overhead", "highest availability"
- Flag uncertain questions and return after completing the rest

## 4.9 Registering for the Exam

1. Go to [aws.amazon.com/certification](https://aws.amazon.com/certification)
2. Sign in to your AWS Certification Account
3. Click **Schedule New Exam**
4. Choose your exam and testing provider: **Pearson VUE** or **PSI**
5. Choose delivery: **Testing Center** (in-person) or **Online Proctored** (at home)
6. Select your date and time, then pay

**Online proctored requirements:** quiet private room, clean desk, stable internet, webcam and microphone, government-issued photo ID.

Results are shown **immediately** after the exam. Official score reports arrive via email within 5 business days.
